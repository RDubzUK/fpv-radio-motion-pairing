Prepare FPV visual motion

    
For FPV clips without usable embedded gyro or attitude data, derive relative camera rotation and estimated body Roll/Pitch/Yaw rates here. Embedded motion is checked first and remains preferred. Saved visual data is reused by matching and Motion comparison; StickCam calibration is not needed for this step.

    
These are experimental 3D rotation estimates using approximate lens geometry, not embedded gyro or gravity. Confirm the actual recorded FOV/projection and camera uptilt; stabilization, parallax and crop changes can bias estimates. Fast flips, blur and featureless sky can cause tracking gaps. Usable tracking shows the percentage of samples with quality above 0.1, not match confidence.

    

    
Visual camera geometry · experimental
110° is an initial approximation, not detected camera calibration. Use the FOV of the recorded video after cropping. FOV/projection changes require fresh visual extraction; camera uptilt changes only remap saved rotation. Camera uptilt is in the matching motion settings. Low quality intervals stay gaps; throttle is not inferred from vertical flow. Older 2D cache files are retained, but a fresh extraction is needed for the new 3D estimates.
The chosen extraction Hz is the saved output rate. Intermediate tracking reduces jumps between images; it takes more processing time. Disable it to track only at the output rate. GPU tracking is controlled by Tracking processor in Saved StickCam movement data.

FPV gyro and camera orientation
Matching prefers embedded gyro or rates derived from fused attitude, with visual fallback. Applies to rigid cameras in acro/rate mode. Enter the camera uptilt to correct mixed axes. The default sensor mapping assumes Gyroflow camera axes (right, up, backwards); confirm it for your camera. Absolute gravity and throttle dynamics are not scored.
Mapping uses each X/Y/Z once; lowercase reverses its direction. zXy maps roll = −Z, pitch = X, yaw = −Y before uptilt correction. Gyro-to-video shift is separate from the StickCam pairing offset. Camera profiles are shared across this session; use separate sessions for different mounts.

    

    
Boundary audio compares the first and last 30 seconds. For unconfigured StickCam, motion is skipped and a distinctive shared audio segment can establish a camera-clock offset; filename dates then help shortlist the remaining overlaps. Camera dates are treated as relative clocks, never trusted as correct wall time.

Motor-sound fallback & confirmed-pair study
      
Motor sound is an experimental activity cue, not RPM or pitch/roll/yaw. Wind, voices, distance and other drones can mislead it. Band limits are configurable. Reference studies extract generic scene movement and available audio dynamics, reuse saved gimbal/stick signals, and compare them with embedded or saved visual FPV motion. Repeated short sections vote for timing. Correlated channels from one modality count as one source, with a fixed corroboration bonus from another modality; no weights are fitted to a known pair. Known offsets remain separate from searched offsets.

    Review the pairs
    Download scores CSV
    
    
    Scores measure correlation evidence, not the probability of a correct match. “Strong” needs repeated content support, a distinct timing peak and separation from competing candidates. Audio-first checks shared landmarks and independently agreeing trends; motion checks reliable active axes. One StickCam may pair with multiple FPV segments; each segment keeps its own offset. Review the videos before editing.
    
    
    
    
    Learned camera dates & supporting pairs
      
    Saving an independently checked pair teaches the date difference for its FPV drone/recorder. Confirmed clock differences more than five minutes apart create separate possible recorder groups, including within one recorder label. A clock reset can also explain a new group. Unassigned clips are considered against each group. Rejected pairs count against their date predictions; rejecting one clock alignment keeps other groups and offsets available. Recording dates can be wrong, including 1970, provided relative ordering is preserved. Durations determine overlap, never flight identity.
