Saved StickCam movement data

Calibrate each recording, then derive the whole calibrated set before matching. Movement data is saved on disk and shared by matching and chart review. Unchanged clips are reused. Changing rates only remaps saved inputs; changing calibration, extraction rate or source footage requires fresh derivation.

Ratios use each clip’s detected average frame rate: 30 fps → 15 Hz or 7.5 Hz. Fewer frames reduce stick tracking work, but decoding still takes time and quick inputs can be missed. Fixed Hz uses the Extract setting below. FPV gyro and comparison Hz are independent; comparing at a higher Hz does not restore missing stick samples. Maximum tracking rate: 60 Hz.

FPV gyro and camera orientation

Matching prefers embedded gyro or rates derived from fused attitude, with visual fallback. Applies to rigid cameras in acro/rate mode. Enter the camera uptilt to correct mixed axes. The default sensor mapping assumes Gyroflow camera axes (right, up, backwards); confirm it for your camera. Absolute gravity and throttle dynamics are not scored.

Mapping uses each X/Y/Z once; lowercase reverses its direction. zXy maps roll = −Z, pitch = X, yaw = −Y before uptilt correction. Gyro-to-video shift is separate from the StickCam pairing offset. Camera profiles are shared across this session; use separate sessions for different mounts.

Boundary audio compares the first and last 30 seconds. For unconfigured StickCam, motion is skipped and a distinctive shared audio segment can establish a camera-clock offset; filename dates then help shortlist the remaining overlaps. Camera dates are treated as relative clocks, never trusted as correct wall time.
